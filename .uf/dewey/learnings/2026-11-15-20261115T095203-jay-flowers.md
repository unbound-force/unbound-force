Keep the takeover in the phase checkpoint's inventory classified as environmental, with the child session IDs, so the audit trail explains why implement-phase work lacks a child run. Two timeouts with near-zero output is an infrastructure signal, not a task-difficulty signal; a third delegation of the same prompt at a longer timeout just burns another 20 minutes.
