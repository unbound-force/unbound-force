A quick-tier security fix shipped as prose choreography must record its interim status in the CHANGELOG entry: the principle trade-off (Constitution Principle V closed immediately, Principle VI DE-005 tool extraction deferred) and a bound follow-up issue naming the extraction and its DE-007 seam layers. Quick-tier branches carry no spec or plan, so the CHANGELOG entry is the constitution Conflict Resolution rule's "relevant artifact" — there is nowhere else the written justification can live. Without the record, the interim prose becomes the de-facto mechanism of record, the extraction lands as an untracked intention, and sibling command families remediated for the same vulnerability class end up carrying two different shapes (#1007 tool-routed vs prose choreography). Corroborates learnings determinism-20261026T153203 (spec-time trade-off documentation) and determinism-20261019T125101 (bound removal follow-ups) at the quick-tier change-record level.
