When the same CI job must live in two workflows (push/PR and scheduled), byte-identical step parity can be enforced by a drift test that extracts each copy's `steps:` block (from the job key to the next top-level job key, line-anchored) and compares them byte for byte — keeping pins, flags, and action SHAs in lockstep without refactoring into a reusable workflow. Useful when the refactor would touch a governance-controlled CI surface the change is not allowed to alter. Precedent: TestOsvPluginTransitive_ScheduledStepsMatchCanonical in unbound-force internal/scaffold; the copyable recipe doc is held to pins-only parity instead, because hero repos deliberately rewrite its comments and resolve their own action SHAs.
