An aggregate 'required check' job (if: always(), needs: [<every gated job>], failing when any needed job concluded anything other than 'success' or 'skipped') lets a workflow skip expensive jobs on narrow PRs while still reporting one stable, always-present required status. It solves two GitHub Actions edge cases: a skipped reusable-workflow caller never reports its nested checks, and matrix jobs skip with matrix expressions unexpanded — both of which would otherwise leave a named required check permanently 'Expected'.
