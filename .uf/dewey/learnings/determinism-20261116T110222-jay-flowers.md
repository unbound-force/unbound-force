A spec that mandates command-prose relaying of a tool-assembled resume hint into a retry converts its own advisory boundary into mechanical prose: once the command prescribes the branch conditions, the once-only bound, the fallback anchor, and the stop conditions, the retry chain is judgment-free and DE-005 applies to the shipped text regardless of the spec's intent. Route bounded retries through the delegation tool instead (the post_feedback_replies shape: the tool owns retry, failure-stop, and ordering; composed text arrives as a parameter) — and amend the spec's command-prose sentence in the same change so requirement and implementation stay aligned.
