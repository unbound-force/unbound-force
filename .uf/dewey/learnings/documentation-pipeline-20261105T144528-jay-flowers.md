Quick-tier bug-fix PRs that change user-facing tool contracts or scaffold migrations owe the full documentation gate, not just the in-repo updates: CHANGELOG, AGENTS.md, and command-doc edits do not satisfy it alone — a docs-labeled tracking issue in the current repo plus the website-repo tracking issue (constitution Cross-Repo Documentation rule) must exist before merge, with the blog/tutorial disposition recorded at filing (the unbound-force/unbound-force#856 and unbound-force/website#317 patterns).
