A quick-tier security remediation that intentionally resolves Constitution Principle V (Security by Default: fix the injection now) over Principle VI (Deterministic Execution: extract the procedure to a tool) MUST record that trade-off and the tool-extraction follow-up in its change record — CHANGELOG entry or PR body — naming the follow-up issue. Without that record, the interim prose choreography becomes the de-facto mechanism of record and the constitution's Conflict Resolution rule (trade-offs require written justification, no implicit principle priority) is silently violated. Observed on PR #1024: the CHANGELOG entry documented the interim form with no trade-off note and no follow-up reference.
