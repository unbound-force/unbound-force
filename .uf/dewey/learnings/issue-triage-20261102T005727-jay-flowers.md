Phased-program issues that share a label are distinct work items when their deliverables differ; triage should compare deliverables and cross-references before classifying topical overlap as duplication. In the unbound-force OpenCode v2 migration, #797 (umbrella orchestration), #798 (Phase 0 spike: compat and surface audit), and #799 (Phase 0 strategic spec) all carry the opencode-v2-migration label, but #799's deliverable is unique — the governance artifact of record that supersedes #714 decision 4 (issues-only tracking) and must document #798's spike findings. Distinguishing signals for phased siblings: an explicit umbrella reference, a "supersedes" decision citation that records the decision trail, and a findings-source citation linking the commissioned artifact to its evidence producer.
