An RFC-style issue that states its purpose, documents an options exploration with elimination rationale grounded in verified technical facts (e.g., direct schema or SDK inspection cited in the body), proposes a concrete implementation plan with a recommended path, and explicitly enumerates its open questions should be triaged VALID rather than NEEDS-CLARIFICATION. Enumerated research unknowns are the design agenda the RFC initiates, not missing information; NEEDS-CLARIFICATION is reserved for absent or ambiguous intent or missing required context. A caller-side duplicate search that returns only the issue itself further supports VALID, and an in-body recommended path that aligns with the repository's own governance posture (e.g., determinism-first restart orchestration) corroborates actionability.
