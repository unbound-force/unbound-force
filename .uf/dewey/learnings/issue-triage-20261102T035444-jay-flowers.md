Triage pattern for RFC-style issues: an issue that enumerates explored options with explicit elimination verdicts, grounds its recommendation in verifiable evidence (schema inspection, SDK surface), and explicitly lists its open questions is fully triageable as VALID (enhancement) — the open questions are design inputs destined for the RFC discussion and the spec clarify phase, not missing requirements. Reserve NEEDS-CLARIFICATION for issues whose intent, scope, or expected outcome cannot be determined from the content itself. Verified on unbound-force issue #645 (session-relocation RFC): seven open questions, yet intent, recommendation, and integration points were complete enough to schedule.
