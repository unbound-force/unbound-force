Learning files harvested from dispatched review-council children can arrive truncated mid-sentence: five of the six council iteration-1 learnings committed in PR #955's chore-dewey commit (64457c88) end mid-word ("...after i", "...then subm", "...the push-fa", "...exactly when t", "...git push <re"), losing each insight's conclusion. Before committing a learnings harvest, verify each body terminates in a complete sentence; a body ending mid-word means the child's structured proposal was cut at submission or harvest time and should be re-stored from the source proposal or completed by hand. Check at harvest time, not review time — a reviewer sees the damage only after it is committed.
