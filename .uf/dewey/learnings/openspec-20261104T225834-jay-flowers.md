When an OpenSpec change amends requirements of an unarchived predecessor by restating the amended clauses under ADDED Requirements (the only option when openspec/specs/<capability>/ does not exist yet), the precedence sentence — "where a clause here conflicts with RD-FR-0NN through RD-FR-0MM, this change governs" — lives only in the change directory and is dropped when the change archives. The archived main spec then carries both the superseded clauses and their replacements as equally governing text. Obser
