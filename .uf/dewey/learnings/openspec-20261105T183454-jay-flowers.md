An OpenSpec proposal's Constitution Alignment section written from memory of the constitution's themes — rather than from its principle list — produced four mislabeled headings (Development Workflow duties such as Spec-Driven Development, Review Council Authority, and Knowledge Preservation promoted to core principles), a duplicate Deterministic Execution entry, and four unassessed real principles (Composability First, Observable Quality, Testability, Security by Default) in a change whose entire purpose was the governance record. Observed on the retroactive-push-skill-contracts proposal (PR 948 round 3). Always enumerate the section headings directly from the referenced constitution version's principle list before writing the assessments; a mislabeled compliance section cannot demonstrate coverage of all active principles.
