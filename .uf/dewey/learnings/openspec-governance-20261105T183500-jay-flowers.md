When authoring a retroactive OpenSpec record (or any artifact carrying a Constitution Alignment section), read the principle headings back from .specify/memory/constitution.md instead of composing them from memory. In PR 948's retroactive-push-skill-contracts proposal, the section assessed against phantom principles ("II. Deterministic Execution", "III. Spec-Driven Development", "IV. Review Council Authority", "V. Knowledge Preservation", "VI. Deterministic Execution (Execution Surfaces)") while the constitution v1.4.0 actually reads I. Autonomous Collaboration, II. Composability First, III. Observable Quality, IV. Testability, V. Security by Default, VI. Deterministic Execution — four real principles were never assessed and the cited precedent (accept-variantless-parent-model) used the correct six. The misnamed section became a review finding in the very artifact created to discharge a governance finding.
