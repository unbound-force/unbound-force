Design-mandated work outside the change's core capability (compensating CI controls, cross-change reconciliation edits, test removals) must be enumerated in tasks.md with verification steps. Untasked work skips the change's verification gate: the task list is what reviewers and the archive step audit, so unlisted work is unverifiable and reads as drift between design and tasks.
