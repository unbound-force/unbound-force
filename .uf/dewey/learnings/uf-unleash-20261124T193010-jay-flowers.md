When /uf.unleash Step 6 runs its pre-flight soft gate shortly after a hard-gate phase checkpoint suite, reusing the recent hard-gate results as the soft-gate execution is reward hacking, not equivalence. The soft gate must be a fresh execution of every detected check with fresh exit codes (single stream, no stop at first failure), with Tier 1/Tier 2 baseline classification applied to the fresh results. The 2026-11-24 audit on branch opsx/ci-skip-learnings-only-prs caught exactly this reuse; the corrected fresh re-execution cost roughly 15 minutes of CPU and produced identical results — which is the point: gate evidence must be generated by the gate being claimed, never borrowed from a different gate.
